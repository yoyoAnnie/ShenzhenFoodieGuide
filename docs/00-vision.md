# Vision

## One-liner
A local's guide to eating in Shenzhen, organized by situation, powered by what people write rather than the stars they click.

## The problem
- Residents don't know where to go for different occasions (date, client dinner, group of 8, solo, late-night).
- Late-night workers can't trust opening hours and struggle to find good deals.
- Existing platforms (Dianping, Meituan) compress experience into a star score that a few angry or bad-mood reviews can drag down without valid reason.
- Outsiders say "Shenzhen has no good food." Locals know that's wrong but have no shareable proof.

## Core insight
Platforms answer "is this restaurant good?" Residents ask "where should I go tonight, with these people, with my taste?" So: **recommend by fit, not by universal score.**

## Principles
1. Text over numbers: meaning lives in what people say.
2. Personal fit over universal rank.
3. Preset tags only, so data stays clean and communities stay comparable.
4. Curate first, automate later: hand-verified data before ML.
5. No paid ranking (protects trust).

## Jobs to be done
- When I'm planning an occasion, I want a trustworthy shortlist so I don't scroll 40 reviews.
- When I leave work at 11pm, I want somewhere good that is actually open.
- When a visitor says Shenzhen has no good food, I want something to send them.
- When I log what I ate, I want the app to learn my taste so next time it's right.
